0.1.4 - 2026-10-26
- Progression expanded on user direction: 9 attributes in three build groups (Warrior: Might,
  Endurance, Precision; Thief: Agility, Guile, Cunning; Mage: Intellect, Wisdom, Willpower),
  each gating exactly two skills, with per-point effect tables.

0.1.3 - 2026-10-26
- Item rarity changed from five to six bands (0-5) on user direction: the rarity index is
  now the enhancement count (0 enhancements at Common, 5 at Runeforged). Five enhancement
  categories therefore cap an item at five enhancements; rarity 5 optionally carries one
  unique runeforged effect that is not an enhancement.
- Updated rarity weights (55/25/11/6/2.5/0.5), socket table, pity ladder (20/80/400 kills),
  rarity-upgrade costs, boss wording and GAME-DESIGN balance table.

0.1.2 - 2026-10-26
- L1 economy rewritten as event-driven (E-00..E-07): income and costs are measured per
  combat encounter, loot event, discovery and boss kill instead of per hour; no idle or
  passive generation; exploration has its own faucet (locations, nodes, caches) and is
  allowed to be quiet.
- L1 player-experience targets reframed from minutes to encounter counts; pacing section
  renumbered (E-08..E-11).
- GAME-DESIGN anti-exploit wording aligned with the event-driven rule.
